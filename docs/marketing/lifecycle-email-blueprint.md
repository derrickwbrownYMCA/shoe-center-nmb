# Shoe Center lifecycle email blueprint

This plan uses consented Shopify customer data only. It does not assume permission to text customers or import legacy POS contacts.

## Capture tags now available in the development theme

- `newsletter`
- `email-subscriber`
- `fit-notes` for the main newsletter
- `carolina-drift-interest` for Carolina Drift updates

## Recommended launch order

### 1. Welcome series

- **Email 1 - immediately:** Welcome, store promise, address/hours, paid pickup, and fit-help phone number.
- **Email 2 - day 2:** How to choose a walking shoe; link to the walking guide and current online assortment.
- **Email 3 - day 5:** Staff-selected categories: On, everyday comfort, men's favorites, and dance-ready styles.
- **Email 4 - day 9:** Verified Google review proof and an invitation to visit or reply with a fit question.

Primary measures: first-purchase conversion, store visit intent, guide clicks, and unsubscribes.

### 2. Browse and cart recovery

- Browse reminder after 6-12 hours when consent and Shopify automation eligibility allow it.
- Cart reminder after 2-4 hours; repeat once after 24 hours.
- Lead with the product and fit help, not artificial urgency.
- Restate $7.95 shipping under $200, free standard shipping at $200, paid pickup, and 14-day store credit.

Primary measures: recovered orders, contribution margin, and support contacts.

### 3. Post-purchase care and review

- **Day 1:** Order/pickup expectations and contact information.
- **Day 7:** Fit check and care guidance appropriate to the purchased product.
- **Day 14:** Store-credit deadline reminder only when operationally appropriate.
- **Day 21:** Google review request with a direct listing link.

Do not offer incentives conditioned on a positive review.

### 4. Replacement and new-model reminders

- Segment by brand/model and intended use when that data is reliable.
- Send useful check-in messages rather than claiming a fixed replacement date.
- Notify prior buyers when an updated model arrives, with a clear explanation of what changed.

### 5. Carolina Drift updates

- New color or verified restock announcements
- Local dance or Shoe Center event notices
- Fit guidance and styling content
- No reservation or preorder language unless the business intentionally introduces paid preorders later

## Required safeguards before activation

- Confirm which existing Shopify and POS contacts have marketing consent.
- Keep SMS disabled until explicit SMS consent and a compliant sending platform exist.
- Verify every policy statement against the live store before scheduling.
- Add UTM parameters and test purchase attribution.
- Send internal test messages before enabling each automation.
