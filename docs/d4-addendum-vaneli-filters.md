# D4 Addendum — Re-add Brand filter + fold in Vaneli colors

**When:** now that Vaneli is published, the catalog is multi-brand again.
**Where:** Shopify admin → Apps → **Search & Discovery** → **Filters**
(https://admin.shopify.com/store/bhnfcj-9i/apps/search-and-discovery/filters)
**Why manual:** the app has no API and its embedded admin iframe blocks
automation (same as the original D4 task). ~10 minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Re-add the Brand filter

1. Click **Add filter** → source **Vendor** → label it **Brand**.
2. Drag it into the filter list (suggested position: after Color, before Size).
   Now shoppers can filter On vs. Vaneli vs. Shoe Center NMB.

## Step 2 — Extend the Color groups with Vaneli's values

Open the **Color** filter → the 7 groups you built in D4 already exist. Add
each new Vaneli color value into the right group below. **Two new groups**
are recommended because Vaneli brings metallics and warm brights that On
didn't have.

### Black  (add)
Black, Black/Black Patent, Black Suede, Black Nappa, Black Knit, Black Beige

### White & Ivory  (add)
Natural White, Offwhite Knit, White Nappa, Bone, Ecru, Clear, Shell Mercury/ Clear, Opal

### Neutrals & Sand  (add)
Taupe, Taupe Dune, Tobacco, Natural Cork, Champaigne, Champagne, Camel,
Camel Suede/Perla, Camel Mini Leopard, Nude Napa, Perla Harmon, Tan Athena,
Sahara Alpine Suede, TMoro

### Blues  (add)
Navy, Ocean, Jean

### Greens  (add)
Turquoise
> (Turquoise straddles blue/green — put it wherever you'd expect to find it;
> Greens is the call here unless you'd rather it sit in Blues.)

### NEW group — Metallics
Silver, Silver Specchio, Silver Mercury, Platino Specchio, Platino Tolosa
> Alternatively, rename the existing **Grey** group to **Grey & Metallic**
> and drop these in there to avoid an eighth group.

### NEW group — Reds & Brights
Red, Orange Suede, Fuchsia

## Step 3 — Save & verify

1. Save in the app.
2. Open the Vaneli collection and a mixed search; the filter panel should
   show **Brand, Color (grouped), Size, Width** with no single-count orphan
   color values.
3. Confirm On's collection still shows the same clean groups (you didn't
   remove anything, only added).
